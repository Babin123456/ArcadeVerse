Investigator's Survival Guide

The Premise:

A boy wakes inside Blackwood Manor on Halloween night. The front door is sealed shut, strange whispers echo down the halls, and a dark entity lurks in the shadows.

Exploration & Puzzles:

  • Navigation: Use the top Room Tabs to move between discovered rooms.
  • Hotspots: Move your cursor/touch over objects in the room to inspect them. Collect keys, diary entries, and tools.
  • Inventory: Tap an item to select it, then interact with locks and mechanisms.
  • Flashlight: Keep light on to inspect dark corners, but watch battery depletion. Find spare batteries in the kitchen.

Surviving Paranormal Encounters:

When a cold chill sweeps through the room and the panic vignette pulses red: Immediately tap the "HIDE" button or turn off your flashlight! Failing to hide before the entity arrives causes severe sanity damage.

Multiple Endings:

Depending on whether you escape through the storm door or cleanse the ritual altar, you will discover one of multiple distinct story endings!
