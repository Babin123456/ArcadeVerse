How to Play (PA Review)

  • Objective: Uncover all safe tiles without detonating any hidden quantum mines.
  • First-Click Safety: Your very first click is guaranteed to be safe and initiates recursive flood fill.
  • Numbers: A number shows how many mines are adjacent in the 8 surrounding cells.
  • Flagging Mines: Right-click on desktop, or toggle the Flag Mode on touch devices.
  • Victory: Reveal every single non-mine tile on the grid to win!
