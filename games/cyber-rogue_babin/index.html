How to Play: Cyber Rogue Protocol

Movement: Use WASD or Arrow Keys (or the on-screen D-Pad) to move your probe 1 tile per turn. Press Space or the center button to wait a turn.

Map Entities:

  • @ (Cyan Probe): Your player character unit.
  • <!> (Red Security Drone): Patrols and attacks within line-of-sight. Bump into bots to attack them.
  • [K] (Gold Keycard): Unlocks the security exit portal for the current floor.
  • [+] (Cyan Data Cache): Restores 25 shield energy points and grants nano-credits.
  • [H] (Green Nano-Stitch): Restores 35 health points.
  • [E] (Exit Terminal): Unlocks after finding the floor keycard to descend deeper!
