Castaway Survival Guide

The Crash & Objective:

You survived a plane crash on an uncharted tropical island. Manage your four vitals (Health, Hunger, Thirst, Energy), gather resources, construct shelters, and find a way to escape back to civilization.

Vitals & Status:

  • Health: Drops from starvation, dehydration, wild boars, or storms.
  • Hunger: Depletes over time. Eat coconuts, berries, or hunt fish/meat.
  • Thirst: Drink from the freshwater stream or crack fresh coconuts.
  • Energy: Rest at a campfire or sleep inside a constructed shelter.

Controls:

  • Movement: WASD or Arrow Keys (or Touch D-pad).
  • Harvest / Attack: Spacebar or [Act] button.
  • Crafting Menu: [C] key or [Craft] button.
  • Consume Food / Water: [E] key or [Consume] button.
  • Rest / Sleep: [R] key or [Rest] button near a shelter or campfire.

Escape Routes:

1. The Seaworthy Raft: Craft a raft and launch from the shore.
2. The SOS Radio Beacon: Salvage the plane's battery and erect an antenna on the high ridge!
