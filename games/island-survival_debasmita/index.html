How to Play: Cursed Island

The Survival Objective:

You are trapped on a haunted island under the Blood Moon. Maintain the sacred Hallowed Pumpkin Bonfire at the island's center and survive the onslaught of nightmare monsters!

Day/Night Cycle:

  • Twilight Phase (30s): Monsters are dormant. Scour the island shores to gather Cursed Driftwood and Soul Embers.
  • Blood Moon Phase (30s): The crimson eclipse begins! Zombie Scarecrows and Shadow Bats assault your bonfire. Repel them!

Controls:

  • Movement: WASD or Arrow Keys (or On-Screen Touch D-pad).
  • Attack: Spacebar or Touch Attack button (hurls Pumpkin Fireballs).
  • Fuel Bonfire: Press F or Touch Fuel button when near the Bonfire. (Consumes 2 Wood to replenish flame).
  • Place Ward: Press E or Touch Ward button. (Consumes 2 Embers to place an explosive pumpkin trap).

Critical Warning:

If the Bonfire fuel drops to 0%, the protective light circle will collapse and the encroaching shadow mist will swiftly devour you!
