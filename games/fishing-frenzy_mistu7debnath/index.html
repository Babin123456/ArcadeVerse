HOW TO PLAY

Learn the mechanics, master the bite timing, and explore the aquatic species.

1

Cast Your Line

Press SPACE or tap CAST. Your bobber arcs into the lake and begins floating naturally.

2

Watch for Nibbles

Fish swim beneath the surface. Subtle ripples appear as a curious fish inspects your bait. Stay alert!

3

Strike on the Bite

When STRIKE! flashes and the timing ring shrinks, press SPACE quickly within the sweet-spot!

4

Build Combos

Consecutive catches multiply your bonus score up to x5. Perfect timing yields bonus points!

FISH FIELD GUIDE & SCORING

COMMON • 10 PTS

Small Minnow

Abundant and nimble. Easy bite window (1.4s). Great for warming up your reaction time.

COMMON • 25 PTS

Blue Perch

Vibrant lake predator with deep cyan tiger stripes and spiny fins (1.2s window).

UNCOMMON • 50 PTS

Golden Carp

Shimmering golden scales and flowing fins. Requires sharp timing (1.0s window).

RARE • 80 PTS

Spiny Pufferfish

Inflates with spines when surprised! Fast and tricky bite reaction (0.85s window).

RARE • 110 PTS

Rainbow Trout

Swift, acrobatic swimmer featuring an iridescent lateral flank (0.75s window).

LEGENDARY • 200 PTS

Legendary Koi

Ancient master of fortune. Lightning-fast bite (0.65s). Yields massive points!
