How to Play: Cyber Brick Breaker

Objective: Clear all colored brick barriers using your energy paddle to bounce the ball without letting it drop below the baseline.

  • Cyan Bricks (1 Hit): Standard data cells (10 pts).
  • Magenta Bricks (2 Hits): Reinforced firewall nodes (25 pts).
  • Gold Bricks (3 Hits): Armored quantum mainframe cores (50 pts).
  • Explosive Red Bricks: Detonates all adjacent blocks in a chain explosion!

Power-Up Capsules:

  • ⚡ Multi-Ball: Spawns duplicate energy spheres.
  • 🔫 Laser Cannon: Equips paddle with blasters (Press Space).
  • 🛡️ Safety Forcefield: Deploys a bottom barrier preventing 1 ball loss.
  • ↔️ Extended Paddle: Expands deflection surface width.
