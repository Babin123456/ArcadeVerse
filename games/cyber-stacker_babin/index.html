How to Play & Controls

  • Drop Block: Press Spacebar, Enter, or tap/click anywhere inside the canvas.
  • Slicing Physics: Any part of the block hanging over the edge is sliced off and falls away.
  • Perfect Placement: Align within 3 pixels of the block below to earn a PERFECT! combo streak and recover block width.
  • Game Over: Miss the tower completely and the simulation terminates.
  • Progression: Stacking speeds up gradually as altitude climbs into the stratosphere.
