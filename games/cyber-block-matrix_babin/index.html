Operation: Cyber Block Matrix

Objective: Guide descending polyomino blocks into complete horizontal matrix lines. Complete lines disintegrate and award Arcade points.

Scoring Matrix:

  • Single Clear (1 Line): 100 × Level
  • Double Clear (2 Lines): 300 × Level
  • Triple Clear (3 Lines): 500 × Level
  • Matrix Tetris (4 Lines): 800 × Level

Special Techniques:

  • Ghost Projection: A translucent neon silhouette projects the exact landing placement of the current falling piece.
  • Hold Buffer (C): Swap the active tetromino into the hold slot for tactical preservation. You can only hold once per drop.
  • Hard Drop (Space): Instantly slam the active piece into position with zero drop delay.
