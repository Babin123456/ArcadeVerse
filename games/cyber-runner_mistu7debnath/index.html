THREAT IDENTIFICATION

Ground Barrier ACTION: JUMP

Low electrified neon barricade. Jump to clear safely.

Overhead Laser ACTION: SLIDE

High-energy pulsating laser beam. Slide underneath.

Firewall Blocker ACTION: STEER

Full-height impenetrable cyber drone. Switch to an open lane!

Energy Core ACTION: COLLECT

Glowing cyan core. Awards +50 bonus points and tracks coins.

SCORING FORMULA

Score increases with distance traveled (1 point per meter) plus 50 bonus points for every energy core harvested. Speed ramps up smoothly over time—test your reflexes!
