FIELD MANUAL: CORE PROTOCOL

1. MISSION OBJECTIVE

Rogue cyber bots advance along the neon conduit towards your Energy Core Vault on the right. Prevent bots from reaching the Core. If the Core Integrity drops to 0%, the sector collapses and the protocol fails.

2. DEFENSE ARSENAL

PULSE TURRET (100 CR)

Balanced firing speed and damage. Reliable foundation for perimeter chokepoints. Effective across all bot classes.

RAPID BLASTER (125 CR)

Ultra-high rate of fire with lighter plasma bolts. Essential for intercepting high-speed Scout drones before they break through.

NOVA CANNON (175 CR)

Heavy antimatter mortars that detonate in devastating Area-Of-Effect (AOE) shockwaves. Shreds clustered groups and armored mechs.

3. IDENTIFIED HOSTILE CLASSES

BASIC DRONE

Standard recon assault drone. Moderate velocity and baseline shielding. Bounty: 15 Credits.

SCOUT SKIMMER

Extreme speed lightweight infiltrator. Sprints past slow-firing cannons. Deploy Rapid Blasters to counter. Bounty: 20 Credits.

TITAN MECH

Heavy reinforced armor plating. Slower movement but massive health pool and core impact damage. Bounty: 40 Credits.

4. ECONOMY & UPGRADES

Earn credits by eliminating hostile bots and successfully clearing waves. Click any deployed turret on the grid to inspect its stats:

  • Upgrade: Enhances damage output, increases targeting range, and accelerates firing cycle up to MK. IV.
  • Recycle / Sell: Recovers 70% of the total credits invested in the turret.
