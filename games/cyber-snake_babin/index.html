How to Play

  • Objective: Guide the cyber snake to collect glowing energy bits without colliding into your own tail.
  • Controls: Use W / A / S / D, Arrow keys, or the on-screen touch D-Pad.
  • Power-Up Bits:
    • Cyan Energy Bit: +10 Pts, +1 length
    • Golden Quantum Bit: +50 Pts, temporary double points
  • Screen Portals: Passing through canvas edges warps the snake smoothly to the opposite boundary!
