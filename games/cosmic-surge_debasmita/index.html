Starship Navigation Move your mouse or drag your finger anywhere on screen to smoothly steer. You can also use WASD or Arrow Keys.
Laser Cannons & Auto-Fire Your blasters automatically target forward while moving. Hold Spacebar or click to unleash rapid-fire bursts!

Threats: Asteroids & Drone Fleets Dodge or destroy tumbling asteroids and incoming drone raiders. Crashing into hazards consumes 1 shield charge.
