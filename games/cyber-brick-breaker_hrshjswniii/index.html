Multi-Angle Deflection Physics

The ball's rebound trajectory depends strictly on where it strikes your energy paddle:

  • Center Zone: Clean vertical bounce for steady control.
  • Mid-Curvature: 45° angled deflection ideal for penetrating inner brick columns.
  • Outer Edges: Steep 72° rapid angle with speed boost for blitzing top corners!
