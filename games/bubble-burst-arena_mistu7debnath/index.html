Core Objective

Aim and launch colored bubbles into the honeycomb cluster. Form groups of 3 or more matching colors to pop them. Complete sector objectives before running out of shots or allowing bubbles to cross the red Danger Line.

Physics & Avalanches

  • Wall Ricochet: Shots bounce accurately off left and right perimeter boundaries. Use wall banks to reach hidden clusters.
  • Ceiling Anchorage: Bubbles remain attached to the ceiling or to connected cluster neighbors.
  • Avalanche Cascades: Any bubbles cut off from ceiling connection will drop freely, rewarding +250 bonus points each!
  • Combo Multiplier: Consecutive successful matches multiply your points up to 5x. Missing a match resets the combo.

Special Arsenal Bubbles

Explosive Nova Bomb

Detonates on contact, clearing all adjacent bubbles in an explosive shockwave (+150 pts/bubble).

Prism Rainbow Bubble

Universal wildcard! Matches with any neighboring color to complete 3+ bubble combinations.
