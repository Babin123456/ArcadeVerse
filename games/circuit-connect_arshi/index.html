How to Play

  1. Click or tap any tile (except fixed power & destination) to rotate it 90° clockwise.
  2. Create a continuous path of pipes from the ⚡ Power Source to the ◎ Destination.
  3. Connected segments light up; when the full path is complete the level is solved.
  4. Fewer moves and faster times earn higher scores.
  5. Use the Hint button if you get stuck — it will highlight a tile that needs rotating.
